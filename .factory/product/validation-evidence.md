# Validation Evidence — SaaS Pipeline OS

## Current validation status

Status: Concept validated internally, not yet externally validated.

## Evidence source

- Rapid SaaS Growth Playbook Notion entry.
- Development Factory Blueprint readiness.
- Existing workflow friction across Notion, ChatGPT, GitHub, Vercel, and Codex.
- Repeated need for structured handover packs, repo planning, and task generation.

## Primary target segment

AI-native solo builder using:
- Notion for idea capture
- ChatGPT/Codex for reasoning and execution
- GitHub for implementation
- Vercel for preview deployment
- A governed development workflow

## Primary pain statement

For AI-native solo builders who frequently convert ideas into small SaaS or agentic product experiments, the transition from raw idea to validated pain, scoped MVP, and implementation-ready GitHub task plan is a recurring problem because the process is fragmented across tools and depends heavily on ad-hoc prompting. Today they solve this with manual notes, custom prompts, Notion pages, and GitHub issues, but this is inconsistent and makes it easy to build before the pain is real.

## Fake-pain risks

- The product may solve Dominik's workflow but not a broader market.
- The pain may be addressed by better prompting rather than a product.
- Builders may prefer flexible ChatGPT conversations over structured UI.
- Direct integrations may be less valuable than simple Markdown outputs.
